How to Add a Remote Node

  1. Get the other party's node information:
    • Their full website URL (e.g., https://their-app.herokuapp.com/)
    • Their service username and password for node-to-node communication
  2. Fill in the form with their information
  3. Click "Add Remote Node" to establish the connection
  4. Ask them to add your node with your information on their side
  5. Once both sides are connected, you can follow each other using the explore page
