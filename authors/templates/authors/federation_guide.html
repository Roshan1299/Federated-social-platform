How to Connect Your Node

Step 1: Share Your Node Information

To connect with another node, you need to exchange information with your connection partner:

  • Your Base URL: {{ base_url }}
  • Service Username: The username you created for node-to-node communication
  • Service Password: The password for the service account

They will need this information to add your node to their system.

Step 2: Add Their Node

Your connection partner should give you their information. Add their node to your system:

  1. Go to Configure Remote Node
  2. Enter their base URL, service username, and password
  3. Click "Add Remote Node"
Step 3: Follow Each Other

Once both nodes are connected, you can follow each other:

  1. Go to the Explore Page
  2. Use the "Follow a Remote Author" form
  3. Enter the full API URL of the person you want to follow
  4. Format: https://their-node.herokuapp.com/api/authors/<uuid>/
Step 4: Approve Follow Requests

When someone follows you from another node, you'll need to approve it:

  1. Check your Follow Requests page
  2. Find requests from remote users
  3. Click "Approve" to accept the follow request

Troubleshooting Tips

  • Connection Issues: Make sure both nodes have each other added as remote nodes with correct credentials
  • Follow Requests Not Working: Verify that the service accounts exist and have correct passwords
  • Posts Not Showing: Check that follow requests have been approved and both nodes are properly configured
  • Need Help: Contact your connection partner to verify all configuration details match on both sides
